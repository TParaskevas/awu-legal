Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

"Ancient World Unboxed Uploader" (the "App") is a private tool used only by the owner of the Ancient World Unboxed channel to publish their own videos to their own TikTok account and to read that account's video statistics. It is not offered to the public.

What data the App accesses

  • Basic TikTok account info (open ID, display name) to confirm which account is connected.
  • The list of the owner's own videos and their public statistics (views, likes, comments, shares).
  • Video files and captions that the owner chooses to upload.

How data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to upload the owner's videos and to produce analytics for the owner's channel.
  • Access tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer and are never published or shared.
  • Statistics may be saved locally in the owner's private notes.

Sharing

The App does not sell, rent or share any data with third parties, and does not use data for advertising.

Retention and deletion

The owner can revoke the App's access at any time in TikTok settings (Security → Manage app permissions). Local tokens and data are deleted when no longer needed.
